2025

  1. Youming Li, Chen Jia*. Effective Markovian dynamics method for solving non-Markovian dynamics of stochastic gene expression. Physical Review Letters (Editors' Suggestion) 135(4):048401, 2025.

    In this work, we develop a novel method for solving a large class of non-Markovian gene expression models with complex protein degradation mechanisms. Our method is applied to obtain analytical results for some important models that fail to be solved using conventional queueing theory. Our theory also successfully explains why non-exponentially degraded proteins on average have smaller mRNA-protein correlation than exponentially degraded proteins.

  2. Bingjie Wu, Chen Jia*. Parameter inference and nonequilibrium identification for Markov networks based on coarse-grained observations. Physical Review Letters 134(8):087103, 2025.

    In this work, we establish a theoretical framework of parameter inference and nonequilibrium identification based on coarse-grained observations for a general Markov network with an arbitrary number of states and arbitrary coarse-grained partitioning. We provide a criterion for evaluating whether the coarse-grained information is enough for estimating all transition rates and also a criterion for detecting whether the system is in a nonequilibrium steady state.

2024

  1. Chen Jia, Ramon Grima*. Holimap: an accurate and efficient method for solving stochastic gene network dynamics. Nature Communications 15(1):6557, 2024.

    In this work, we develop an accurate and highly efficient method for solving stochastic gene network dynamics. The gene network can be very general, including transcriptional networks, post-transcriptional networks, and post-translational networks. Comparing with the classical Gillespie algorithm, our method can reduce computational time by over 60 fold. Within the same computational time, our method can enhance the accuracy by over 3 fold.

  4. Bingjie Wu, James Holehouse, Ramon Grima*, Chen Jia*. Solving the time-dependent protein distributions for autoregulated bursty gene expression using spectral decomposition. The Journal of Chemical Physics 160(7):074105, 2024.

    In this work, we give the complete spectral characterization and obtain the exact time-dependent solution for a coupled gene circuit with positive-plus-negative feedback and translational bursting. The generator matrix of the corresponding Markovian model is an unbounded non-self-adjoint operator. We prove that the eigenvalues of the generator matrix satisfy a nontrivial continued fraction equation and the associated eigenfunctions are Heun functions with four regular singularities.

  2. Chen Jia*. A solution to the reversible embedding problem for finite Markov chains. Statistics & Probability Letters 116:122-130, 2016.

    The embedding problem for finite Markov chains is a long-standing open problem in probability theory and stochastic processes, first proposed by the mathematician Elfving in 1937. In this work, we completely resolve the problem in the reversible special case. Specifically, we establish necessary and sufficient conditions for both the existence and uniqueness of reversible embeddings, and we further develop an explicit algorithm for constructing all such embeddings.
